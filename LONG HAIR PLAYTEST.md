# Longer hair — first fit

Hellgirl now uses the two hair sections extracted from the supplied Helena Harper OBJ, fitted to her existing head and tinted brown. The hairstyle reaches approximately to her shoulders. The rest of Helena's mesh was discarded from the working character.

The player loads Content/HellgirlTest/Hellgirl_LongHair. It uses the same skeleton and retains the facial shapes and body materials. Body secondary motion is unchanged. Hair simulation is paused; the scalp follows the head, and the lower sections share some chest weight to reduce shoulder penetration. Full long-hair physics and collision are not set up yet.

Editable character: Developer idea folder lol/Stage 01 Hands/Assets/Player Character - Hellgirl/Model/Purchased_Base/Hellgirl_LongHair_v01.blend. The preview PNG is beside it. The imported hairstyle has 3,838 vertices and 6,303 polygons, with two masked materials. The downloaded originals and previous character versions are retained.

The Blender fit was visually inspected. Unreal import preserved the skeleton and facial morphs, and the game rebuild passed. Check the hairstyle during turns, running, and kicks for clipping; it remains a first fit.

The supplied Imp FBX and textures were located under Enemies - Demon 01 Imp/hand-painted-imp. They have not been integrated into the enemy actors in this hair pass.
